Privacy notice

HoneyDo is an owner-operated Mac application for reviewing tasks, connected records and outgoing messages. This notice describes its current connected features. This public website is separate from the private application.

Google information accessed

  • Gmail: read selected messages and their context, and send a finished message only after the owner approves it.
  • Contacts: read names and available contact details, including email addresses, phone numbers, organization information and photos, to select an exact recipient.
  • Calendar and Tasks: read calendar/task lists and selected events or tasks to prepare work for review.
  • Drive: read file metadata and, when selected, a supported document's text or preview. The app does not currently write Drive files.

Google grants are account-specific. Read requests are bounded and do not establish complete coverage of an account. Local sources such as Notes metadata and saved family-app actions are checked separately.

How information is used

Information supports the owner's visible task list, source context, contact selection and message preparation. Accepting a task is separate from reading a source. Finishing a local task does not finish or change the original record. HoneyDo does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence models.

Storage and retention

The private workspace, selected source excerpts, task history, drafts, message review records, preferences, signatures and selected attachments or pictures are stored on the Mac. Google tokens and client credentials are kept in macOS Keychain. Local copies and backups can remain until the owner removes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorized reads but does not automatically erase saved local material.

Sharing and sending

HoneyDo communicates with the connected provider to perform the authorized operation. Approved outgoing email is transmitted through Gmail to the exact reviewed recipients with the approved content and attachments. The app does not sell connected information, use it for advertising or automatically upload it to this website. Its current connected features do not send Google records to an external AI model.

HoneyDo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

The owner can stop using a connection and revoke HoneyDo's access through Google Account connections. Local records and backups must be removed separately on the Mac. To request help reviewing or removing HoneyDo's local data, contact brockgorubec@gmail.com.

This website

These information pages contain no private task or communication records, sign-in forms, analytics scripts or application credentials. The hosting provider processes ordinary web requests under its own privacy practices.
