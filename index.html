Know the next step.

HoneyDo brings selected tasks and connected information into a compact list, with the details beside the work. It is a private, owner-operated Mac application, not a public signup service.

Connected context

Read authorized Gmail, Google Contacts, Calendar, Tasks and Drive information. Selected records can become tasks after review.

Messages you approve

Prepare email with the sending account, recipients, message, signature and attachments visible. The owner approves the exact finished package before it sends.

Kept on the Mac

The workspace is stored locally. Google authorization credentials are kept in macOS Keychain. This website does not contain the private workspace.

Access is specific

Connections are checked separately. Reads can be limited to a page of results or an existing saved snapshot. Receiving information currently happens when the owner refreshes a source; this is not an unattended inbox or a complete record of every app.
